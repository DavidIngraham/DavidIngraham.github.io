About · David Ingraham

A little curiosity
goes a long way.

I’m David. I’m an engineer living in the Columbia River Gorge, and I like seeing where a good question takes me.

01 Following a question

I like finding out for myself.

A lot of my projects start with “I wonder if…” I’ll make something, try it, and see what happens. Sometimes it works. Sometimes the result sends me back to the question with a better idea of what I’m missing. I enjoy that part, too.

That curiosity shows up in flying robots, control systems, and tools that turn terrain into something you can hold. I like understanding how things work, but I’m happiest when I can put an idea to the test and learn something I didn’t expect.

02 At home in the Gorge

There’s plenty to explore out here.

I moved to the Columbia River Gorge for work. I stayed because I love being outdoors here, and the balance it brings to my life.

Some of my favorite experiences are simply good days outside with good company. I value the room those days leave for noticing things, trying something new, and having a conversation that takes its time. Helping care for trails is one small way I take part in a place that gives me a lot.

03 Learning with people

The best conversations wander a little.

I like conversations where we get past the quick answer and start working something out together. A passing comment can turn into an unexpectedly deep discussion. Those are often my favorite conversations.

I love it when a conversation leaves both of us with a new question.

I enjoy encouraging that curiosity in other people, whether it’s a spontaneous conversation or mentoring someone over time. It’s rewarding to help someone find their own way into a problem—and to discover how much I still have to learn along the way.

A few questions have turned into projects.
